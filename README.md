# 📊 Customer Intelligence Dashboard (Tableau)

## 📌 Project Overview

This Tableau dashboard was developed to analyze customer behavior, revenue contribution, profitability, and concentration risk using the Superstore dataset.

The objective was to transform transactional data into actionable business insights that help decision-makers understand customer value, identify high-performing segments, and reduce revenue dependency risks.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Addressed

* Which customer segments generate the highest revenue and profit?
* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Is revenue concentrated among a small group of customers?
* Which customers contribute positively or negatively to profitability?
* How do customer-driven sales trends change over time?

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### 1. Customer Segment Performance

Analyzes sales and profit contribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customer segments.

### 2. Top Customers

Identifies the highest revenue-generating customers and highlights key business relationships.

### 3. Customer Profitability

Evaluates customer-level profit performance to identify highly profitable and low-performing customers.

### 4. Customer Concentration Analysis

Measures dependency on top customers and highlights concentration risk within the customer base.

### 5. Customer Value Trend

Tracks sales performance over time to identify growth patterns, volatility, and customer-driven trends.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### Segment Performance

The Consumer segment contributes the highest share of sales and profit, making it the primary revenue driver for the business.

### Customer Concentration Risk

A relatively small group of customers generates a significant portion of total revenue, indicating concentration risk and the need for diversification.

### Revenue Drivers

Top-performing customers contribute disproportionately to business growth and should be prioritized through retention and relationship management initiatives.

### Sales Trend Analysis

Monthly sales demonstrate noticeable fluctuations, suggesting the presence of seasonality or external demand factors that warrant further investigation.

### Profitability Opportunities

Customer-level profitability analysis helps identify opportunities to improve margins and optimize resource allocation.
